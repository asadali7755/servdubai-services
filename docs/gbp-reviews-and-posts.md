### 2. Who and when

- Ask **every** customer, the same day, after the job is done. Google does
  not allow asking only the happy ones.
- The technician asks in person at the end ("If you are happy with the
  work, a Google review helps us a lot"), then the office sends the
  WhatsApp message below within an hour.
- One reminder after 2-3 days if there is no review. Then stop.

### 4. Rules (Google policy; breaking them can remove all reviews)

- No discount, gift or free service in return for a review.
- No reviews from staff, family or friends, and none written for a customer.
- Do not ask only happy customers, and do not ask anyone to change or
  remove a review.
- One review per customer; never post from the same phone or Wi-Fi for
  several customers.

### 5. Replies (owner, within 24-48 hours, to every review)

Mention the service and area naturally; it helps the profile show for
those searches. Never copy the same reply twice in a row.
